Biking Club

Jane Doe - example@fcstu.org

The biking club will be a club of people who like to bike. During club week meetings we will talk about bikes or trails, why we bike, and other related subjects. We will also plan out of school group riding trips on mountain bike trails, the greenway, or on a road route.
